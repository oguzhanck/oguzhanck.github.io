Privacy Policy for Purr Blocks

Purr Blocks: Cat Block Puzzle ("the game") is made by Oğuzhan Çakmak, publishing as Nomad Applications, who is the data controller. Contact: feedbacknomad@gmail.com. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, on what legal basis, who receives it and what your rights are.

What we use, why and on what basis

  • Your game. Your board, scores and record, yarn, cats, day streak and settings are saved on your device only. The game has no accounts and no online backup; we do not receive your name, email address or contacts. Purpose: running the game. Basis: performing our agreement with you (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Gameplay statistics. Events such as games started and finished, lines cleared, ads offered and watched, notifications opened and the first steps of a new player, with an installation ID that Google Firebase creates for the game, your device model, operating system, language, app version and country or region (from the internet address), sent to Google Firebase Analytics. Firebase Remote Config also downloads the game's settings with that installation ID. Purpose: understanding how the game is played and improving it. Basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 5(2)(f)). They start only after the notice shown at the first launch, and you can turn them off at any time: Settings > Privacy Settings.
  • Error reports. When the game crashes or meets an error, the error and device information with the installation ID, sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Purpose: fixing problems. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Advertising ID and ad interactions. The game has no forced ads: an ad is shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads come through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network), which use the advertising ID, approximate location (from the internet address), device information and how you interact with ads to show, measure and protect ads. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent screen at the first launch, and you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy Settings. In Türkiye, ads are picked for you (personalized) only with your explicit consent, which the game asks separately from the terms, right before the first ad you choose to watch (see the consent text); until then, and without it, ads are not personalized. Elsewhere ads may be personalized unless you turn this off in Settings > Privacy Settings. On iPhone, Apple's tracking question also applies. Basis: your consent for personalized ads (Art. 5(1)); showing the rewarded ad you ask for in exchange for its reward, performing our agreement (Art. 5(2)(c)).

Notifications are planned and shown on the device; nothing is sent to us for them, only the fact that you opened the game from one is counted in the statistics. The game has no in-app purchases: nothing in it is sold for real money.

Who receives it

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), processing on our behalf.
  • Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network), which use ad data for their own advertising purposes.

These companies' servers are outside Türkiye (for example in the United States and the EU), so your data is transferred abroad. Türkiye has no adequacy decision for these countries, and your data may not be protected at Türkiye's level there. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Your game stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Gameplay statistics are kept for up to 14 months and error reports for 90 days, then deleted. The ad networks keep ad data under their own policies.

Children

The game is made for adults and is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you may: learn whether your data is processed; ask for information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; know the third parties it is transferred to, in Türkiye or abroad; ask for it to be corrected; ask for it to be deleted; ask that a correction or deletion be passed on to those third parties; object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR also gives you the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, take your data with you and withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

In the game, Settings > Delete my data deletes everything the game keeps on your device and gives the game a new installation ID, so the gameplay statistics already sent are no longer linked to you; uninstalling the game deletes what is on your device too. For anything else, write to feedbacknomad@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days, free of charge. If your request is refused or not answered, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within 30 days of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request. You can also reset the advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date below shows the latest version. If purchases come with an update, this policy will say what they need before they are offered.
